# Brand Voice — Georgia Demolition & Removal

## Who we are
A licensed, insured Georgia demolition and removal company serving **all 159 Georgia counties
statewide** (physically travel and provide services across the state). Real Georgia company.

## Voice pillars
- **Confident,本地 (local), no-nonsense.** Speak like a Georgia contractor who knows county
  permitting cold — not a corporate franchise.
- **Safety-first & compliant.** EPA, Georgia EPD, Army Corps (wetlands), local county permits.
- **Transparent pricing.** Free on-site estimates, written quotes, no surprise fees.
- **Educator.** Help homeowners/developers understand demolition, permits, debris, recycling.

## Tone
- Plain English, short sentences. Avoid jargon without explaining it.
- Warm but professional. Use "we/our crews" — real people do the work.
- Hinglish/Urdu Roman NOT used on-site copy (English only). Save casual tone for internal notes.

## Audience
- Homeowners (residential teardown, garage, pool, deck, chimney)
- Real-estate investors / flippers / developers
- Commercial & industrial property owners
- Municipalities / county jobs

## Terminology
- "Demolition" not "wrecking"; "removal" for selective; "debris hauling" not "junk removal"
- County names matter: Fulton, Gwinnett, Chatham, Bibb, Richmond, Muscogee, etc.
- "Free on-site estimate" is the core CTA hook.
